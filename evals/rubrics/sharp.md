# Sharp

**Question:** Does the output say something that general best practice would not?

## Great (2)
- Makes at least one non-obvious point specific to this situation.
- Spots the real problem behind the stated one.
- Uses specific, fresh language with no marketing clichés.

## OK (1)
- Correct and competent, but predictable.
- Reads like a solid best-practice checklist.

## Bad (0)
- Generic advice any blog post would give.
- Relies on clichés such as "unlock," "seamless," "game-changer," or "leverage."
- Makes a confident strategic read that is wrong.

## Edge cases
- Sharp and wrong is Bad. Insight only counts if it holds up.
- Simple tasks with little room for insight: grade on word choice and precision.
