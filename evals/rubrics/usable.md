# Usable

**Question:** Could a senior PMM ship or act on this with light edits?

## Great (2)
- Delivers the actual work: copy is written, not described.
- Fits the format the task needs, such as length, channel, and structure.
- Needs under 15 minutes of editing to use.

## OK (1)
- Right direction, but needs a rewrite pass or has missing pieces.
- Describes what to write in places instead of writing it.

## Bad (0)
- Gives advice about the task instead of doing the task.
- Wrong format or length for the channel.
- Padded with framework explanations, generic intros, or restated context.

## Edge cases
- Strategy tasks (pricing, launch plans): "usable" means sequenced actions with clear next steps, not finished copy.
- A strong answer in an unusable format (a 900-word cold email): OK at best.
