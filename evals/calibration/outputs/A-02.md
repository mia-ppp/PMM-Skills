# A-02

## Prompt

We sell an AI tool that drafts first-pass SOC 2 evidence from engineers' Jira and GitHub activity. Buyers compare us to Vanta and Drata. Deals stall because prospects say they already have Vanta. We're a 12-person startup. How should we position?

## Output

## Recommended positioning

For compliance teams at growth-stage startups who already run Vanta or Drata, [Product] is the AI layer that turns engineers' actual Jira and GitHub activity into audit-ready SOC 2 evidence — so engineers stop getting pulled into audit fire drills to manually explain their own work.

This is deliberately *not* "another Vanta." You're not selling a replacement GRC platform to a company that just bought one. You're selling relief from the specific, recurring pain that Vanta/Drata don't solve: engineers manually translating their own commits, PRs, and tickets into auditor language every audit cycle.

## Trade-offs we are making

- **We give up the first-time SOC 2 buyer entirely.** That deal wants one vendor and one bill. Don't chase it — you'll lose it to Vanta/Drata on breadth every time, which is exactly the stall you described.
- **We give up "single platform" deals.** Anyone who wants vendor consolidation is not your buyer right now. Say so explicitly rather than trying to out-argue it.
- **Smaller initial ACV per account than a platform play**, in exchange for a sales cycle that doesn't require displacing an incumbent.
- **We drop "compliance platform" language entirely from messaging.** Any copy that reads like "AI-powered SOC 2 automation" invites direct Vanta comparison and reopens the stall. Everything should say "works with Vanta/Drata," never "instead of."

## Stress-test results

- **Swap test — fails partially, flagged.** Could Vanta say "we draft first-pass evidence narratives from engineers' Jira/GitHub activity"? Unclear — Vanta already integrates with GitHub/Jira for evidence collection. The differentiation rests entirely on whether they draft *narrative, control-mapped evidence* from that data or just surface raw logs/screenshots. **This is the single fact that would most change this positioning** — verify it before finalizing messaging.
- **Proof test — fails, currently.** No time-savings or win-rate evidence cited. Every value claim above is marked `Need`. This positioning is directionally right but not yet ship-ready for a sales deck.
- **Rep test — passes.** "We're the AI layer that turns your engineers' Jira and GitHub work into audit-ready SOC 2 evidence, so they stop re-explaining commits to auditors — even if you already run Vanta or Drata." One sentence, no notes needed.
- **Buyer test — passes.** Uses "audit-ready evidence," "auditor evidence requests," "fire drill" — language pulled from the stated pain, not internal product vocabulary.

## Assumptions and what would change this

I did not find a `.agents/product-marketing-context.md`, so this is provisional based on what you told me. Assumptions made:

1. **Vanta/Drata collect raw evidence but don't draft audit-narrative text from engineering activity.** If they already do this well, your differentiation collapses to "we do it better," which is a much weaker, feature-level claim — you'd need a head-to-head comparison, not a complementary frame.
2. **The stalled deals are mostly with existing Vanta/Drata customers**, not prospects evaluating GRC tools for the first time. If a meaningful share of stalls are first-time buyers choosing Vanta over you, your best-fit segment is wrong and needs re-scoping.
3. **Engineering leadership, not just the compliance/security owner, feels acute pain.** If engineers don't actually complain about audit evidence work today, the "stop the fire drill" value prop won't land — worth checking against actual sales call notes or win-loss data.
