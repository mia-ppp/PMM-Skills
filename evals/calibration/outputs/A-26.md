## Prompt

Help me position a new stablecoin payouts API for marketplaces paying international sellers. Competitors are Stripe Connect, Payoneer, and Wise Business. Our edge is same-day settlement in 40+ countries with no FX markup.

## Output

I didn't find a `.agents/product-marketing-context.md`, so I'm assuming:
1. Best-fit customer is gig/freelance and creator marketplaces paying high volumes of individual sellers in emerging markets (not B2B supplier marketplaces or US/EU-only platforms).
2. "Stablecoin-settled" is acceptable in your technical story even if it's not the headline buyer-facing phrase — some marketplace finance/compliance buyers are still crypto-wary.

## Recommended positioning

For marketplaces paying international sellers, [Product] is the payouts API that settles same-day in 40+ countries with no FX markup — so sellers get paid faster and keep more of what they earn than they would through Stripe Connect, Payoneer, or Wise.

## Positioning canvas

| Element | Choice |
|---|---|
| Best-fit customer | Gig/freelance and creator marketplaces with high-volume, recurring payouts to individual sellers concentrated in emerging-market corridors, where payout speed and take-home pay directly drive seller retention |
| Competitive alternatives | Stripe Connect (deep platform integration, dev experience, but slower cross-border settlement + FX spread); Payoneer (marketplace-native, seller wallets already adopted, but variable settlement + FX cut); Wise Business (transparent FX messaging, but not built as a marketplace payout API and not stablecoin-speed) |
| Contextual alternative | Batching payouts monthly/quarterly to reduce FX/ops cost, manual wires via treasury/ops staff, or restricting which countries sellers can be paid from |
| Unique capabilities | Same-day settlement across 40+ countries via stablecoin rails; true no-markup FX conversion; no need for marketplace to pre-fund/hold local currency reserves |
| Value (with "because") | Sellers get paid same day regardless of country, because payout speed is a trust/retention signal for gig and creator sellers who will switch platforms over slow pay (Need: settlement-time benchmark vs. competitors). Sellers keep more of every payout, because FX markup is the top cross-border seller complaint on comparable platforms (Need: effective FX cost comparison). Marketplace finance teams skip local banking/treasury setup to launch new countries, because treasury complexity is a stated blocker to geographic expansion (Need: customer quote or case study) |
| Market frame | Claim the existing "marketplace/cross-border payouts" category; own the subcategory "stablecoin-settled, same-day marketplace payouts." Full category creation is not justified yet — no evidence buyers are ready to evaluate outside the Stripe/Payoneer/Wise frame |
| Proof | Have: none cited yet. Need: settlement-time data vs. competitors, effective FX-cost comparison, at least one design-partner case study or seller retention data point |

## Trade-offs we are making

- We are not positioning for marketplaces whose sellers are mostly US/EU-based — speed and FX savings matter less there, so this pitch will underperform against Stripe Connect's broader platform story.
- We are not competing on payments+payouts unification. Stripe Connect wins if the buyer wants one API for the whole money flow; we win only on the payout leg.
- We are deprioritizing B2B supplier marketplaces, where payout cadence and relationship terms matter more than same-day speed.
- Leading with stablecoin infrastructure (even if downplayed in headline copy) will cost us some deals with compliance-conservative buyers who don't want crypto rails anywhere in their stack — we're accepting that trade rather than diluting the differentiation.

## Stress-test results

- **Swap test:** Partially fails as written. Wise could plausibly claim "no FX markup" today (their brand is built on transparent fees), so that half of the claim isn't yet differentiated — it needs sharpening to something Wise specifically cannot say, e.g., mid-market-rate stablecoin conversion with a named cost comparison. "Same-day in 40+ countries" passes — none of the three can currently claim that combination.
- **Proof test:** Fails today. Every value claim is marked "Need." This positioning is provisional until settlement-time and FX-cost data exist.
- **Rep test:** Passes. "We give marketplaces one API to pay international sellers same-day, without the FX cut Stripe Connect or Payoneer take."
- **Buyer test:** Mostly passes — "sellers," "same-day," "FX cut" are buyer language. "Stablecoin-settled" is internal/technical vocabulary and should stay in the technical docs, not the headline position.
