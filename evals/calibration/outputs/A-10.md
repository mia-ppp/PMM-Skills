# "Mailchimp Alternative" Landing Page Strategy

Here's a full page outline plus copy suggestions you can adapt to your product.

## SEO/Intent Notes First
People searching "mailchimp alternative" (singular) are typically:
- Currently using Mailchimp and hitting a wall (pricing, features, e-commerce limitations)
- Comparison shopping before switching
- Looking for validation that switching is worth the hassle

Your job: acknowledge their pain point, position as the e-commerce-specific solution, make switching feel easy.

---

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero Section
**H1:** The Mailchimp Alternative Built for E-Commerce Brands

**Subhead:** Stop forcing your store into a generic email tool. [Product Name] connects natively to Shopify/WooCommerce and turns your customer data into revenue—without the Mailchimp price hikes.

**CTA:** Start Free Trial / See Live Demo
**Secondary CTA:** Compare Plans

*Include a trust bar: "Trusted by 5,000+ e-commerce brands" + customer logos*

---

### 2. The "Why Switch" Section
Address specific Mailchimp pain points with a scannable comparison:

| Pain Point | Mailchimp | [Your Product] |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing scales with contacts (even unengaged ones) | ✗ | ✓ Pay for active subscribers only |
| Native e-commerce automation | Limited | ✓ Built-in abandoned cart, post-purchase flows |
| Segmentation by purchase behavior | Basic | ✓ Advanced RFM/product-based segments |
| Support response time | Slow (free tier) | ✓ Live chat/priority support |

*(Fill in with your actual differentiators—be honest, don't oversell)*

---

### 3. E-Commerce-Specific Feature Blocks
Structure as 3-4 modules, each with a screenshot/GIF:

- **Abandoned Cart Recovery** — "Recover carts automatically with sequences that adapt to product type"
- **Post-Purchase Flows** — "Turn one-time buyers into repeat customers"
- **Revenue Attribution** — "See exactly which emails drove sales, not just opens"
- **Segmentation** — "Target by purchase history, LTV, or product category—no manual tagging"

---

### 4. Migration Section (critical for conversion)
This is often the #1 objection. Address it directly:

**H2:** Switching from Mailchimp takes less time than you think

- One-click import of contacts, tags, and campaign history
- Free migration support (dedicated Slack/email channel)
- Templates auto-converted
- "Most brands are fully migrated within [X days]"

---

### 5. Social Proof
- 2-3 case studies specifically from brands that *switched from Mailchimp*
- Include specific metrics: "Switched from Mailchimp, increased email revenue 34% in 60 days"
- If possible, get a quote that mentions Mailchimp by name (approved for use)

---

### 6. Pricing Transparency
Mailchimp's biggest complaint is pricing opacity/scaling. Be blunt:

- Show a comparison calculator: "Enter your subscriber count, see what you'd pay on Mailchimp vs. us"
- Or simple pricing table with "vs. Mailchimp at this tier" callouts

---

### 7. FAQ Section (great for SEO + objection handling)
- "Is switching from Mailchimp difficult?"
- "Will I lose my automation/flows?"
- "Can I import my Mailchimp templates?"
- "How is pricing different from Mailchimp?"
- "Do you integrate with [Shopify/WooCommerce/BigCommerce]?"

---

### 8. Final CTA
**H2:** Ready to leave Mailchimp behind?
CTA + risk reducer ("No credit card required" / "Free migration included")

---

## SEO Technicals
- **URL:** `/mailchimp-alternative` (singular, matches intent)
- **Title tag:** `Mailchimp Alternative for E-Commerce Brands | [Product]`
- **Meta description:** ~155 chars, include "mailchimp alternative" naturally
- **H1** should contain exact phrase once
- Add schema markup for FAQ section
- Internal link from comparison/blog content targeting adjacent terms ("mailchimp vs klaviyo," "mailchimp pricing")
