## Where Asana Went Wrong (For Small Teams)

Asana isn't a bad tool. It's just not built for you anymore. Here's the bloat we hear about most:

**1. Too many views for too small a team**
List, Board, Timeline, Calendar, Gantt, Workflow — great if you have a dedicated ops person to maintain them. Overkill if you're just trying to ship work.

**2. Admin complexity**
Custom fields, rules, forms, and approval workflows require setup time most small teams don't have. You end up hiring "someone who's good at Asana" instead of just using the tool.

**3. Notification overload**
Small teams report drowning in status updates, @ mentions, and auto-generated activity logs — noise that makes it harder, not easier, to know what's actually happening.

**4. Enterprise pricing for enterprise features you don't need**
You're subsidizing features like advanced reporting, portfolios, and goals tracking that are built for orgs with 200+ employees and multiple departments to coordinate.
