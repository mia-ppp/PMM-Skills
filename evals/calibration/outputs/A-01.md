# A-01

## Prompt

Here's my SaaS landing page: https://example.com/product. We get about 5,000 visitors/month from Google Ads but only 1.2% convert to free trial signups. Can you help me figure out what's wrong?

## Output

I'd love to dig into this with you — quick heads up though: I can't actually browse to the URL directly, so I'll need you to share some specifics so I can apply the CRO framework properly. A 1.2% conversion rate on paid traffic is a signal something's off, but let's diagnose rather than guess.

**First, let me check if there's existing context** — do you have a `product-marketing-context.md` file with your positioning, ICP, and messaging? If so, share it and I'll use it instead of asking you to repeat things.

**To analyze the page, could you share:**
1. The headline and subheadline (exact copy)
2. Your primary CTA button copy
3. A screenshot or description of the hero section layout
4. What trust signals you currently have (logos, testimonials, review scores, etc.)
5. Roughly how long the page is / what sections it includes (features, pricing, FAQ, etc.)

**And a few diagnostic questions to narrow down where the leak is:**

1. **What are your Google Ads keywords/ad copy?** — Message match between ad and landing page is one of the most common silent killers. If your ad promises "X" and the page opens with something different, visitors bounce immediately.
2. **What's your bounce rate on this page?** (If you have GA/analytics) — High bounce = value prop or message match problem. Low bounce but low conversion = CTA, trust, or friction problem further down.
3. **What does the signup flow look like after the CTA click?** — Sometimes the landing page is fine and the leak is in the form itself (that'd point to a different fix).
4. **Do you have any session recordings or heatmaps** (Hotjar, FullStory, etc.)? Even a handful would tell us a lot — where people stop scrolling, whether they're even seeing your CTA.
5. **What's considered a "good" benchmark for you** — is 1.2% way below a previous number, or has it always been around there?
6. **Is this page used for multiple ad campaigns/keywords, or one specific campaign?** — A generic landing page trying to serve many search intents is a common conversion killer for paid traffic.

Once I have the actual copy/structure, I'll run it through the full framework (value prop clarity, headline, CTA hierarchy, visual hierarchy, trust signals, objection handling, friction points) and give you prioritized fixes — quick wins, high-impact changes, and test ideas.

In the meantime, one thing worth checking right now: **pull up 5-10 of your actual Google Ads search terms/queries** and compare them to your headline. If there's a gap between what people searched and what the page says, that's very likely a big chunk of your problem.
