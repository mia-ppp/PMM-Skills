# A-07

## Prompt

Rewrite this headline: 'An Innovative AI-Powered Platform for Streamlined Business Operations' — it's for a B2B SaaS tool that helps small businesses manage invoicing and payments.

## Output

Before I lock in the final version, a couple of quick things would help me sharpen it:

1. What's the #1 outcome your customers care about most — getting paid faster, spending less time on admin, or avoiding late payments/chasing clients?
2. Any proof point I can use (e.g., "get paid 2x faster," "cut invoicing time to 5 minutes")?

In the meantime, here's why the original doesn't work and some strong directions:

**What's wrong with it:**
- "Innovative," "AI-Powered," "Streamlined" — pure buzzwords, zero specificity
- "Business Operations" is vague — doesn't tell me it's about invoicing/payments
- No benefit, no outcome, no reason to care

### Rewrite Options

**Option A — Outcome-focused:**
> **Get Paid Faster. Chase Invoices Never Again.**
> Send invoices, collect payments, and track cash flow — all without the back-and-forth.

*(Rationale: leads with the transformation, uses "never again" formula to hit the pain point directly)*

**Option B — Time-savings angle:**
> **Invoicing for Small Businesses, Minus the Busywork**
> Create, send, and collect on invoices in minutes — not hours.

*(Rationale: specific, honest contrast — "minutes not hours" is concrete and believable)*

**Option C — Question-based:**
> **Tired of Chasing Clients for Payment?**
> Automate invoicing and get paid on time, every time.

*(Rationale: rhetorical question engages the reader's own frustration immediately)*

**Option D — Direct/plain-spoken:**
> **Simple Invoicing. Faster Payments.**
> The easiest way for small businesses to bill clients and get paid — no accounting degree required.

*(Rationale: strips it down to the two things customers actually want, no fluff)*

---

All four drop "AI-powered," "innovative," and "platform" — none of those are benefits, they're just company-speak. If AI is a genuine differentiator (e.g., auto-categorizing expenses, predicting late payments), tell me and I can work it into a subheadline instead of the headline — it'll land better as a *proof point*, not the hook.

Which direction feels closest to your brand voice — punchy and direct, empathetic/pain-focused, or plainspoken and simple? I can tighten from there.
