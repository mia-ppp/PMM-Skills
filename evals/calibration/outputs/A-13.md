# A-13

## Prompt

Help me position a new stablecoin payouts API for marketplaces paying international sellers. Competitors are Stripe Connect, Payoneer, and Wise Business. Our edge is same-day settlement in 40+ countries with no FX markup.

## Output

# Positioning Strategy: Stablecoin Payouts API for Marketplaces

## Core Positioning Statement

**"The payout infrastructure that pays international sellers like they're local — same-day, every day, without the hidden FX tax."**

For marketplaces scaling cross-border seller networks, [Product] uses stablecoin rails to deliver same-day settlement in 40+ countries with zero FX markup — while competitors make sellers wait 3-5 days and silently eat 2-4% on every conversion.

---

## Competitive Wedge: Where Each Competitor Is Vulnerable

| Competitor | Their Strength | Your Wedge |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe Connect** | Best DX, trusted brand | Great API, but payout speed and FX cost weren't designed for high-volume international sellers — you built for that from day one |
| **Payoneer** | Established seller trust, marketplace integrations | Legacy rails = 2-5 day settlement and opaque FX spreads. You're the modern, transparent alternative |
| **Wise Business** | Real FX transparency, low fees | Built for business transfers, not embedded marketplace payout infrastructure — no seller onboarding, KYC orchestration, or API-first payout logic at scale |

**Key insight:** None of these were architected around stablecoin settlement, so none can structurally match same-day + zero markup simultaneously. Speed *or* cost — you offer both because your rails are fundamentally different, not just optimized.

---

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. Speed as a Seller Retention Lever
"Marketplaces don't just compete on product — they compete on how fast sellers get paid. A 3-5 day payout cycle is a churn risk you're accepting by default."

### 2. FX Markup = Invisible Platform Tax
"Every basis point of FX spread comes out of your sellers' margin, not yours — but they blame *you*, not their bank. Zero markup means happier sellers and stronger retention."

### 3. Built for Marketplace Complexity, Not Retrofitted
"We're not a payments company that added crypto. We're built stablecoin-native for multi-country payout orchestration — split payments, mass payouts, compliance-ready KYC in 40+ countries."

---

## ICP & Use Case Framing

**Best-fit buyers:**
- Marketplaces with 30%+ international seller base (freelance, e-commerce, creator platforms)
- High payout volume/frequency (gig, dropship, affiliate models)
- Platforms in emerging markets where local banking rails are slow/expensive (LATAM, SEA, Africa)

**Objection to pre-empt:** "Stablecoins = crypto = risky/compliance nightmare"
→ Reframe: sellers never touch crypto. You abstract stablecoin rails behind a familiar payout experience — they see local currency, instant funds, done.

---
