# A-09

## Prompt

Write homepage copy for a SaaS tool that automates employee onboarding. Target audience is HR directors at mid-size companies (200-2000 employees). Main differentiator is that it integrates with all major HRIS systems and cuts onboarding time from 2 weeks to 2 days.

## Output

# Homepage Copy: Employee Onboarding Automation

**A few assumptions I made** (flag if you want me to adjust):
- Primary CTA is "Book a Demo" (typical for a sales-assisted mid-market HR tool). If this is self-serve, I can swap to a free trial/signup flow.
- Tone: professional but human — HR directors are busy, skeptical of hype, and tired of "revolutionary" software promises.
- Traffic assumed to be mixed (organic + paid), so the page carries the full argument rather than matching one specific ad.

HR directors are numbers-driven and skeptical of buzzwords, so specificity does the persuading. The subheadline immediately answers the objection that's already forming in their head: "but will it work with my system?"

## The Problem

### Onboarding shouldn't take longer than hiring did.

Right now, a new hire's first two weeks probably look like this: paperwork stuck in one system, IT provisioning stuck in another, and your team manually chasing signatures, forms, and approvals across five different tools that don't talk to each other.

Meanwhile, the new hire is sitting there — unpaid attention, no laptop, no idea what to do — wondering if they made the right choice.

> **Annotation:** This section mirrors the internal narrative HR directors already have, using their language ("chasing signatures," "unpaid attention") rather than abstract terms like "inefficient processes." It builds the case before offering the solution, per the Problem → Solution flow.

---

## The Solution

### One onboarding system. Every HRIS you already run.

**Works with the tools you have**
[Product] integrates natively with Workday, BambooHR, ADP, UKG, Gusto, Rippling, and 20+ other HRIS platforms. No migration. No "rip and replace." Your data stays where it lives.

**New hire day one, actually ready**
Paperwork, equipment requests, benefits enrollment, and account provisioning kick off automatically the moment someone accepts an offer — not the moment they show up.

**HR gets one dashboard, not five tabs**
See exactly where every new hire is in the process, what's blocking them, and who needs to act next — without logging into four different systems to find out.

**Consistent onboarding, every time**
No more onboarding quality depending on which recruiter or manager is running it. Every new hire gets the same complete, compliant experience.

**From 2 weeks to 2 days**
Teams using [Product] cut average time-to-productive from 14 days to 2, because tasks run in parallel instead of waiting on manual handoffs.

> **Annotation:** Each benefit is stated as an outcome ("ready on day one," "one dashboard") rather than a feature list ("automated workflows," "API integrations"). The HRIS compatibility is repeated here — it's the differentiator most likely to make or break a demo booking, so it earns its own line instead of being buried in a feature grid.

## How It Works

**1. Connect your HRIS**
Link [Product] to your existing system in minutes — no IT project required.

**2. Build your onboarding workflow once**
Set up tasks, forms, and approvals for each role or department. Reuse it for every hire after.

**3. New hires complete tasks automatically**
Offer letters, I-9s, equipment requests, and training assignments trigger on their own, in order.

**4. Track progress in real time**
See every new hire's status at a glance, and get alerted the moment something needs your attention.

> **Annotation:** Four steps, each starting with a verb, each short enough to scan in under 10 seconds — this section exists to make the product feel simple to adopt, since "will this be a pain to set up?" is a top hesitation for HR buyers evaluating new software.

## Objection Handling

**"We just switched HRIS systems last year. We're not doing that again."**
You don't have to. [Product] connects to your existing HRIS — it doesn't replace it.

**"How long does implementation actually take?"**
Most teams are live within a week, without help from IT.

**"Is our employee data secure?**
[Product] is SOC 2 Type II certified and encrypts all data in transit and at rest. [Link to security page]

**"What if our HRIS isn't on your list?"**
We support 20+ major platforms. If you don't see yours, [talk to us] — we likely already have a path.

> **Annotation:** These are written as the literal questions an HR director would ask a salesperson, not softened into generic FAQ headers. Naming the fear directly ("we're not doing that again") builds more trust than avoiding it.

## Headline Alternatives

- **Option A:** "Cut employee onboarding from 2 weeks to 2 days" *(leads with the hard number — best for a skeptical, ROI-driven audience)*
- **Option B:** "The onboarding tool that works with the HRIS you already have" *(leads with the integration differentiator — best if HRIS compatibility is the top-of-funnel concern in your sales conversations)*
- **Option C:** "Stop babysitting onboarding across five different systems" *(leads with the pain point — best for paid ads targeting frustrated HR ops managers rather than HR directors evaluating strategically)*

## CTA Alternatives

- **"Book a 15-Minute Demo"** *(specific time commitment lowers perceived friction)*
- **"See [Product] With Your HRIS"** *(personalizes the demo promise to their exact situation)*
- **"Get a Free Onboarding Audit"** *(reframes the CTA as a free deliverable rather than a sales call — better if demo requests are underperforming)*
