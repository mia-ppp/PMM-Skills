## Table stakes (not pillars)

These are true and expected, but every serious competitor can say them too — don't lead with them:

- **Security / multi-sig support** — expected of any treasury or custody tool. Belongs on a security page, not the homepage headline.
- **Role-based permissions** — standard finance-tooling feature. Fine in a feature list, not a pillar.
- **API / accounting software integrations** — expected connective tissue, not a differentiator on its own.
- **Ease of use / clean UI** — every vendor claims this. Show, don't headline.
- **Support for major chains** — chain *coverage* is table stakes; chain *consolidation into one real-time ledger* is the pillar.
