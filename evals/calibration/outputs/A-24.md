Our positioning is 'the all-in-one workspace for modern teams.' We keep losing deals to Notion and Linear. Rework it.

## Before / After

| | Statement |
|---|---|
| Before | "The all-in-one workspace for modern teams." |
| After | "The workspace where cross-functional teams turn plans into tracked work — without copying between docs and tickets." |

**What changed and why:** The old line names no customer, no alternative, and no capability — Notion and Linear could both say it truthfully, which is exactly why you lose on the shortlist. The new one names the buyer (cross-functional teams, not engineers or solo knowledge workers), the alternative it beats (the docs-to-tickets translation tax), and a mechanism, not a vibe.

## Recommended positioning
For cross-functional teams who plan and execute work together, [Product] is the workspace where the plan and the tracked work are the same object — so nothing gets lost translating a Notion doc into a Linear ticket.

## Trade-offs we are making
- We stop trying to win pure engineering teams outright — Linear will beat us on speed and eng-specific workflow depth, and that's fine.
- We stop trying to win teams whose primary need is an open-ended knowledge base/wiki — Notion's flexibility wins there, and that's fine.
- We will lose some deals where the buyer wants best-of-breed tools stitched together rather than one opinionated system. We're betting the reconciliation tax is painful enough that this is a shrinking group among our target segment.
- "Modern teams" as a target is gone. That was never a real segment — it was a way to avoid choosing one.

## Stress-test results
- **Swap test:** Could Notion or Linear truthfully say "the plan and the tracked work are the same object"? No, as long as the unified-record capability is real — this is the one claim in the old positioning's place that neither can borrow. **Conditional pass, pending confirmation of the capability.**
- **Proof test:** Fails right now. No proof is in hand; everything is marked `Need`. Do not ship this externally until at least one of the proof points is real.
- **Rep test:** "We're the workspace where your plan and your tracked work are the same thing, so you're not copying from a doc into a ticket." One sentence, no notes needed. **Pass.**
- **Buyer test:** Uses "copying between docs and tickets," a phrase buyers actually say in complaints about Notion+Linair stacks, not internal terms like "unified workspace platform." **Pass.**

## Assumptions and what would change this
1. **Whether Notion losses and Linear losses come from the same buyer or two different ones.** If they're different buyer types, "cross-functional teams" is the wrong unifying segment and you may need two positioning statements, not one.
2. **Whether the unified plan/execution capability is real today** or is roadmap. If it's roadmap, this whole positioning is aspirational and should be labeled internally as such until shipped.
3. **What win-loss notes actually say buyers chose Notion/Linear for.** If it's price, integrations, or a specific missing feature rather than "too much reconciliation work," the core claim here is solving the wrong problem.
