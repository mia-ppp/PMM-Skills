# A-21

## Prompt

we need a page targeting 'mailchimp alternative' (singular). we're an email marketing platform focused on e-commerce brands.

## Output

Before I build the "Mailchimp Alternative" page, I need a few specifics so the content is accurate and not generic. I checked for a `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` file for existing context — I don't see one in this conversation, so let's cover the essentials here (this will also give us a reusable base for future competitor pages).

**About your product**
1. What's your product name and one-line positioning?
2. What are the 3-4 biggest reasons e-commerce brands switch from Mailchimp to you? (e.g., better Shopify/WooCommerce integration, better deliverability, more intuitive automation, pricing at scale, better segmentation)
3. Where are you honestly weaker or not a fit? (e.g., no landing page builder, smaller template library, newer to market — whatever's true)
4. What's your pricing model vs. Mailchimp's, roughly? (especially at the list sizes where Mailchimp gets expensive — this is usually a huge switching trigger)
5. Ideal customer profile — solo DTC brand, mid-market e-commerce, enterprise?

**Proof & support**
6. Do you have any customer quotes/case studies specifically from people who switched off Mailchimp?
7. Do you offer migration support (import lists, recreate automations/flows, etc.)? What does that look like concretely?

**Competitive intel**
8. Anything specific you know about *why* people leave Mailchimp — from reviews, sales calls, or your own switcher research? (Common Mailchimp complaints tend to be: pricing jumps as list grows, contact-based billing counting unengaged/unsubscribed contacts, e-commerce automation feels bolted-on, support quality, deliverability complaints — but I don't want to assume these apply to your positioning without confirmation.)

Once I have these, I'll draft the full page:
- Meta title/description
- TL;DR summary
- "Why people look for a Mailchimp alternative" section
- Positioning summary (you as the alternative)
- Detailed comparison (features, pricing, service) — with a table
- Who should switch (and who shouldn't — being honest here)
- Migration path section
- Switcher social proof
- CTA

If it's faster, you can also just paste in any existing sales battlecards, pricing pages, or competitor research you already have and I'll extract what I need from that instead.
