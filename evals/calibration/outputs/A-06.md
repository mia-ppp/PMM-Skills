# A-06

## Prompt

Here's my SaaS landing page: https://example.com/product. We get about 5,000 visitors/month from Google Ads but only 1.2% convert to free trial signups. Can you help me figure out what's wrong?

## Output

I can't actually browse to that URL or see your live page, but I can give you a systematic framework to diagnose this yourself in the next 30 minutes, plus the most common culprits behind a 1.2% conversion rate (which is below average — decent SaaS landing pages convert 3-5%+ from paid traffic).

## First, check message match (most common killer)

Since traffic is from Google Ads, mismatch between ad copy and landing page is the #1 suspect:

- Pull up your top 5 ad groups and their exact headlines/keywords
- Compare against your landing page H1
- If someone searches "invoice software for freelancers" and lands on a generic "The #1 Business Platform" headline, that's your problem

**Fix:** Headline should mirror the searcher's intent within 3 seconds.

## Second, run this checklist on the page itself

| Element | Question |
|---|---|
| Above the fold | Can I tell what the product does + who it's for without scrolling? |
| CTA | Is it specific ("Start free 14-day trial") vs vague ("Get Started")? |
| Friction | How many form fields? Credit card required? |
| Social proof | Any logos/testimonials visible before the fold? |
| Load speed | Check PageSpeed Insights — anything over 3s kills mobile conversion |
| Mobile | Is the CTA button actually thumb-reachable and not cut off? |

## Third, segment the data you already have

Before changing anything, check in Google Ads + GA4:

1. **Device split** — mobile vs desktop conversion rate (often wildly different)
2. **Ad group performance** — is the 1.2% an average hiding a 4% and a 0.1%?
3. **Bounce rate** on the landing page — over 60% suggests message mismatch or slow load
4. **Search terms report** — are you paying for irrelevant queries that inflate visitor count but were never going to convert?
