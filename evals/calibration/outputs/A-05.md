Write launch messaging for our new AI agent that auto-resolves Tier 1 support tickets in Zendesk. Target is mid-market support leaders.

## Output

# Launch Messaging: AI Agent for Zendesk Tier 1 Support

## Positioning Statement

For mid-market support leaders who are drowning in repetitive Tier 1 tickets, **[Product Name]** is an AI agent that auto-resolves routine Zendesk tickets end-to-end—not just deflects them. Unlike chatbots that hand off frustrated customers to human agents, our AI agent understands context, takes action, and closes tickets with the same quality your best Tier 1 rep delivers.

---

## Core Messaging Framework

### Headline Options

1. **"Your Tier 1 team, minus the burnout."**
2. **"Close tickets. Not just conversations."**
3. **"The AI agent that actually resolves — not just responds."**

### Subheadline
Auto-resolve up to 60% of Tier 1 Zendesk tickets with an AI agent that takes real action: issuing refunds, updating orders, resetting accounts, and closing the loop — all without a human touch.

---

## Key Messages by Audience Pain Point

| Pain Point | Message |
|---|---|
| **Rising ticket volume without headcount** | "Scale support without scaling headcount. Our AI agent handles the repetitive 60% so your team can focus on the 40% that actually needs a human." |
| **Agent burnout / turnover** | "Stop burning out your best people on password resets. Give Tier 1 the boring work back to AI — and give your agents the complex, rewarding work instead." |
| **Slow resolution times hurting CSAT** | "Resolve tickets in seconds, not hours. Customers get instant answers 24/7 — no queue, no wait, no 'let me transfer you.'" |
| **Distrust of AI / fear of bad CX** | "This isn't a chatbot that dead-ends into frustration. Our agent takes real actions inside Zendesk — refunds, order updates, account changes — with full audit trails and human escalation built in." |
| **Cost pressure / doing more with less** | "Cut cost-per-ticket by [X]% while improving resolution speed. Full ROI visibility from day one." |

## Feature → Benefit Translation

| Feature | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Native Zendesk integration | Live in days, not months — no rip-and-replace |
| Action-taking AI (not just suggestion-based) | Tickets actually close — no more "escalate to human" fallback |
| Confidence-based escalation | Only uncertain or sensitive cases reach your agents |
| Full audit trail & explainability | Trust and compliance for regulated industries |
| Real-time analytics dashboard | Prove ROI to leadership with hard resolution & CSAT data |
