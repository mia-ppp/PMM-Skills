# A-19

## Prompt

this page isn't converting. can you take a look? it's our homepage for a B2B project management tool

## Output

I'd be glad to help diagnose the conversion issues. Since I can't actually browse to the live page, I'll need you to share some information with me. The more of this you can provide, the better my analysis will be:

**Most useful: paste the page content/copy directly here**, including:
- Headline and subheadline
- Body copy sections
- CTA button text
- Any pricing/social proof shown

**Also very helpful:**
- A screenshot (I can analyze layout/visual hierarchy if you describe or share it)
- The actual URL, if you want me to reference it even though I can't load it live

**Context that shapes my recommendations:**
1. What's the traffic source? (paid ads, organic, referral, direct) — this changes what "converting" should mean
2. What's the specific conversion goal? (free trial signup, demo booking, waitlist, contact sales)
3. Current conversion rate, if you know it, and what you're benchmarking against
4. Who's the target buyer? (IC/team lead self-serve vs. procurement-driven enterprise sale — this majorly affects what the page needs)
5. Anything you've already tried or suspect might be the issue

A few common failure points I'll be checking for once I see the content:
- **Clarity gap** — visitor can't tell what the tool does or who it's for in 5 seconds
- **Weak differentiation** — sounds like every other PM tool (Asana/Monday/ClickUp clones all say "streamline your workflow")
- **CTA mismatch** — asking for too much commitment too early (or too little for the buyer stage)
- **Proof deficit** — no logos, testimonials, or specific outcomes/numbers
- **Friction** — form length, unclear next steps, no urgency

Paste in what you've got and I'll give you a structured teardown.
