# A-08

## Prompt

We sell an AI tool that drafts first-pass SOC 2 evidence from engineers' Jira and GitHub activity. Buyers compare us to Vanta and Drata. Deals stall because prospects say they already have Vanta. We're a 12-person startup. How should we position?

## Output

# Positioning Against "We Already Have Vanta"

This objection usually means prospects don't understand you're not a competitor to Vanta — they think you're a redundant purchase. That's a positioning failure, not a product failure. Here's how to fix it.

## First, Get the Category Right

Vanta and Drata are **compliance automation platforms** — they manage the audit workflow, monitor infrastructure controls, track policies, and produce the compliance dashboard auditors see.

You are **evidence generation for the human-work side** — the stuff Vanta/Drata can't automate because it lives in unstructured engineering activity (PR descriptions, Jira tickets, code review comments) that requires interpretation, not just API polling.

**Positioning line:** "Vanta tells you what evidence you're missing. We write it."

## Reframe the Comparison

Stop letting prospects put you in a head-to-head with Vanta. Instead:

| Vanta/Drata does | You do |
|---|---|
| Connects to AWS/GCP and confirms MFA is on | Reads 40 Jira tickets and drafts the change-management narrative auditor wants |
| Flags "missing evidence for CC7.2" | Turns that flag into an actual draft ready for engineer review |
| Automates infra control checks | Automates the manual, engineer-time-sink part everyone hates |

The wedge: **Vanta/Drata's biggest complaint in every G2 review is "still requires tons of manual evidence collection from engineers."** You are the fix for their #1 weakness, not a replacement for their strength.

## Sales Motion Changes

1. **Kill the "vs" framing in messaging.** Website copy, sales deck — nothing should say "alternative to Vanta." Say "works with Vanta" or "the missing piece for Vanta customers."

2. **Lead with the pain, not the category.** Don't ask "do you have Vanta?" Ask: "How many engineer-hours did your last audit cycle burn on evidence collection?" That number is usually 40-100+ hours. That's your hook, independent of tooling stack.

3. **Target the right buyer.** Vanta is bought by security/compliance leads. You should also be selling to **eng managers and VPEs** who are tired of their team's time getting eaten by audit prep. Different buyer, different budget line, no perceived overlap.

4. **Build (or fake) the integration story fast.** Even a lightweight "exports to Vanta-ready format" or "reads your Vanta control gaps and drafts against them" collapses the objection instantly. At 12 people, this doesn't need to be deep — it needs to exist and be demoable.

5. **Get a customer quote that says the magic words.** Nothing kills the objection faster than: *"We use Vanta for monitoring and [You] for evidence drafting — they're not the same thing."* One good case study neutralizes 80% of this objection in every future call.

## The One-Liner to Test

> "Vanta and Drata monitor your controls. We do the part they can't automate — turning your engineers' actual Jira and GitHub work into audit-ready evidence, in minutes instead of days."

## Immediate Action

Since deals are stalling *now*, not eventually — audit your last 10 lost deals. If "we have Vanta" killed them at the same stage (e.g., after demo, before pricing), that tells you whether it's a messaging problem (they didn't get the differentiation) or a real feature-overlap problem (Vanta actually does what you do well enough). Those require very different fixes, and worth knowing before you invest more in repositioning.
