# Optional launch platforms

Select a platform only when its audience and launch objective fit. Check current rules and costs with the platform owner; no universal submission or review quota.
Before launch: approved listing description, authentic screenshots/demo, working destination and CTA, availability, reviewer permissions and support coverage. Plan narrative, maker/spokesperson involvement, timing, owner and genuine community outreach.
On launch day: test the experience, respond truthfully, capture feedback and route defects. Do not fabricate engagement or purchase votes.
After launch: follow up with consent, synthesize learning and compare qualified demand, activation and retained value with the objective. Search/listing presence belongs to search-discoverability; reviews to customer-marketing; integration listings to partner-marketing; copy to marketing-copy.
