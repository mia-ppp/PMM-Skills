## Voice

- **Plain over clever.** Say what the product does. Wordplay only in social.
- **Specific over big.** A named rail, license, or partner beats "global," "seamless," or "next-generation."
- **Calm about regulation.** State licenses as facts. No fear-based framing about competitors' compliance.
- **Banned words:** revolutionary, game-changing, seamless (as a lead claim), unlock, Web3 (in enterprise and institutional copy), to the moon, WAGMI.
