# MoonPay canon

Canon version: 2026-09-28
Owner: PMM (single owner; everyone else proposes changes)
Source basis: public sources only (MoonPay newsroom, press releases, developer docs, and named press coverage). Internal MoonPay messaging was not used.

Labels follow `../../_shared/evidence-gaps.md`. **Sourced** items cite the source. **Assumed** items are PMM drafts derived from public material and need owner sign-off.

## 1. Positioning (internal, not customer-facing)

**Company.** MoonPay is a licensed, single-integration network for moving value between fiat and digital assets, for apps, platforms, and financial institutions. *(Assumed. Derived from the April 2026 boilerplate. Owner to confirm.)*

**MoonPay Enterprise.** MoonPay Enterprise is a single-integration stablecoin payments platform for fintechs, neobanks, PSPs, and platforms moving money between fiat and stablecoins. *(Assumed. Derived from the Aug 2026 launch release. Owner to confirm.)*

**Consumer.** MoonPay is the embedded way to buy, sell, and swap crypto with everyday payment methods, for people using wallets and apps. *(Assumed.)*

Derivation trace:
- "Single integration": MoonPay boilerplate, PR Newswire, Apr 23 2026. *(Sourced)*
- "Licensed": BitLicense, NY trust charter, US MTLs, MiCA, same boilerplate. *(Sourced)*
- "Fintechs, neobanks, PSPs, platforms": Virtual Accounts release, Apr 23 2026, and MoonPay Enterprise release, Aug 2026. *(Sourced)*

## 2. External tagline

**"MoonPay is how the world moves value."** *(Sourced: closing line of MoonPay press boilerplate, 2026.)*

Use it as the sign-off line. Never alter it ("how the world moves money" is off-canon).

## 6. Boilerplate (approved)

**One-liner (Assumed):** MoonPay moves value between fiat and digital assets through one licensed integration.

**Company boilerplate (Sourced, adapted from Apr 23 2026 release):** Founded in 2019, MoonPay is a global financial technology company that helps businesses and consumers move value across fiat and digital assets. MoonPay has more than 30 million customers across 180 countries and supports more than 500 enterprise customers spanning crypto and fintech. Through a single integration, MoonPay powers on- and off-ramps, trading, crypto payments, and stablecoin infrastructure. MoonPay is how the world moves value.

Regulatory sentence (add for press, enterprise, and institutional audiences): MoonPay holds a New York BitLicense, a New York Limited Purpose Trust Charter, and money transmitter licenses across the United States, and has MiCA authorization in the EU to provide on- and off-ramps.

## 7. Known drift in public materials (why this canon exists)

MoonPay has described itself at least four ways in 2026 releases: "the leading global crypto payments network," "the leader in global crypto payments and stablecoin infrastructure," "the global financial technology company powering the movement of value," and "a global financial technology company that helps businesses and consumers move value." The canon standardizes on the last one plus the tagline. The "leading" and "leader" framings are retired (see claims.md B1).
