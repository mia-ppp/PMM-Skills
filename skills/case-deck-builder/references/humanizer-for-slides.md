# Humanizer checklist for slide copy

Use this when the Humanizer skill is unavailable. Review wording only. The source document and assignment remain authoritative.

- Replace inflated claims and significance language with the specific finding.
- Remove filler, vague labels, generic praise, and unnecessary intensifiers.
- Prefer natural verbs and concrete nouns over abstract phrasing.
- Vary sentence shapes. Avoid repetitive contrasts and forced groups of three.
- Read headlines and body copy aloud. Keep the tone professional and suited to the audience.
- Do not add first-person reactions, anecdotes, humor, evidence, or conclusions that the source does not support.
- Keep important qualifications, source tags, assumptions, and gaps.
- Preserve exact quotations. Do not silently rewrite quoted source material.
- Do not include a conversational editing audit or humanizer commentary in the deck.

Follow the user, assignment, source-accuracy, and presentation requirements before this checklist.
