# Customer review programs

Invite eligible customers with actual experience, consent and a clear request for honest feedback. Do not cherry-pick only praise, script endorsements, suppress criticism or manufacture reviews. Follow platform rules and disclose permitted incentives.
Coordinate CS timing, suppression, customer permissions, response ownership and issue escalation. Verify current review-platform policies and listing truth.
Measure review quality, representativeness, response/issue resolution and buyer proof value; an arbitrary review quota does not establish success. Approved customer language and reusable evidence route to customer-research, messaging review and sales-enablement.
