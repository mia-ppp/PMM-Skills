# Cancellation cohort guidance

Use cohort differences as hypotheses for retention briefs, not billing instructions. Follow the customer-marketing entrypoint and retention-programs reference. Respect direct cancellation and consent; surveys, offers and CS conversations must not block it.

## Business-model differences

- **Self-serve SaaS:** Offer an optional short reason survey, relevant value-realization help and a direct confirmation path.
- **B2B/team plans:** Coordinate administrator, stakeholder and contractual context with CS. Higher account value may justify optional personal outreach, not blocking self-service or requiring a save call. Operations confirms applicable cancellation terms.
- **Free-to-paid:** Explain an approved downgrade option and what the free tier retains. Preserve the customer's cancellation choice; do not invent entitlements.

## Billing-interval differences

Monthly and annual cohorts may differ in commitment, renewal timing and needs. Validate those differences from evidence rather than assuming price sensitivity or satisfaction.
Pause, downgrade, discount, annual-plan switch, credits or refunds require approved pricing/commercial policy and eligibility. PMM briefs relevant communication; billing/CS owns operations. Do not invent offer amounts, refund terms or access dates.

## Post-cancel experience

Confirmation should state the actual cancellation status, owner-confirmed access end date, approved data-retention policy and supported reactivation path. Use placeholders until operations verifies those facts; never promise a generic 90-day data policy.
A win-back sequence needs consent, relevant evidence that a barrier is resolved, eligibility, suppression and a review window. Timing is a testable proposal, not a universal day-7/30/60/90 schedule. Final sequences belong to email-sequence.

## Cohort intervention hypotheses

| Observed cohort signal | Hypothesis and validation |
|---|---|
| New customer cancelling | First value may be unclear; validate before briefing education or optional setup help with Product/CS. |
| Engaged customer naming price | Test perceived value, budget and fit; do not infer that they love the product or automatically discount. |
| Dormant account | Investigate lost relevance, access barriers or fit; inactivity alone does not establish cause. |
| Buyer naming a competitor | Capture stated reasons and verify capabilities. Brief approved comparison/help; never invent roadmap promises or competitive offers. |

Segment by lifecycle, documented usage, plan, billing interval, previous interventions and stated reason only when useful. Distinguish source language, PMM interpretation and proposed intervention. Measure durable retained value, later cancellation, complaints and learning; route proposed canon changes through human approval.
