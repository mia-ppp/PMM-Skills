# Retention program briefs

Synthesize cancellation reasons by cohort, lifecycle, use case and source. Preserve raw customer wording separately from PMM interpretation; deduplicate and show sample limits, voluntary versus involuntary loss and unknown reasons. Route reusable VoC to customer-research.
Compare value realization, education, fit, price, product reliability and missing capability hypotheses. A selected cancel reason is evidence, not proof of root cause.
Brief interventions with eligible cohort, observed signal, hypothesized barrier, approved message/proof, CS/Product owner, offer eligibility, consent and measure. Health scores and risk cutoffs require calibration, not invented universal weights.
For cancellation, ask a short optional reason survey and respect a direct exit. Offer only approved pauses, support or commercial options; never hide cancellation or create fake urgency. Pricing/discount decisions belong to pricing-strategy and required approvals.
Win-back triggers need relevant evidence of resolved barriers or renewed value, eligibility, suppression, timing and consent. Email-sequence owns final sequences.
Measure retained value and durable retention by cohort and window, not just interrupted cancellations: reactivation, sustained usage, voluntary/involuntary churn, retained revenue, complaints and later re-cancellation. State numerator/denominator and avoid comparing incompatible retention measures.
Return intervention learning, reason changes and evidence gaps to customer research and strategy review. Billing operations owns retry schedules, provider settings and access; request owner-confirmed delivery and support policies for communication briefs.

For business-model, billing-interval and post-cancel cohort differences read [cancellation cohorts](cancellation-cohorts.md). Illustrative offers require commercial approval.
