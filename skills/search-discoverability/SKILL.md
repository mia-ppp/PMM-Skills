# Search Discoverability

## Approved context and evidence

Read `../_shared/evidence-gaps.md` and `../_shared/ssot-consumption.md`.
Load only relevant approved manifest context and record sources in Inputs and assumptions.
Read `../_shared/segment-selection.md` when an audience decision is needed; inherit accepted segments and ICP.
Separate sources, interpretation, and proposals. Missing evidence remains Assumed or Gap.
Do not edit SSOT. Route proposed canon changes to `ssot-context-loop` for human review and approval.

## Modes

| Mode | Load and assess |
|---|---|
| Traditional search visibility | [Traditional search](references/traditional.md): buyer query coverage, intent, on-page relevance, crawl/index diagnosis, internal linking, cannibalization and search visibility. |
| AI/answer-engine visibility | [Answer engines](references/answer-engines.md): documented query sets, platform/date, repeated observations, answer/citation visibility, citation-source analysis, extractable patterns and third-party presence. |
| Scaled/programmatic opportunities | [Scaled search](references/scaled.md): defensible data, unique buyer value, template briefs, cannibalization, pilot selection and indexation monitoring. |

## Workflow

1. Map accepted buyers and evaluation questions to query sets and existing content. Distinguish branded, category, use-case and competitive discovery. Record platform, date, method and observation limits; one answer is not universal visibility.
2. Diagnose coverage, citations and technical impediments separately. Cite actual evidence; mark unavailable ranking/indexation data as Gap. Do not promise ranking or AI inclusion.
3. Prioritize recommendations by buyer value, confidence, cost and dependencies. For scale, require unique value and defensible data before a small pilot; stop expansion if quality, cannibalization or indexation guardrails fail.
4. Brief `content-strategy`, `marketing-copy` or `competitor-alternatives` for content; `website-buyer-journey` for buyer paths; web/SEO engineering for deployment. PMM approves truth and claims, not markup mechanics.
5. Specify leading coverage metrics and qualified acquisition outcomes with `analytics-tracking`, owner and review cadence.

## Required output

Inputs and assumptions; mode/query scope; sourced visibility diagnosis; buyer-coverage and citation gaps; prioritized content/technical briefs; scale pilot and safeguards where applicable; owner handoffs; measurement plan; ranked Evidence gaps.
Final content passes originating requirements and independent messaging review. This skill does not deploy pages or silently redefine strategy.
