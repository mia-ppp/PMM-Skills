# Positioning to messaging trace

Use this reference when a request spans repositioning, positioning and messaging.
It is a lightweight handoff contract, not an extra report requirement for narrow
copy or product-specific requests.

## Keep the levels separate

| Level | Decision | Output | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company | What role the company can credibly earn across its portfolio | Company-level position and narrative | Market change, company strengths, competitive frame, proof boundary |
| Beachhead | Which segment plus business use case to serve first | Solution position and buying committee | Use case, workload, customer problem, trigger, buyer evidence |
| Product | What capability or package addresses that problem | Capability and proof table | Shipped capability, maturity, outcome proof, validation gap |
| Expression | How the position is said to a buyer | Pillars, persona messages, applied copy | Buyer language, alternatives, proof, channel or sales action |

For a broad repositioning, show how the beachhead is an evidence-backed way to
test the company position. Name adjacent segments or use cases only as
sequenced hypotheses. Each one needs its own ICP, buyer, alternatives,
positioning, product, GTM and evidence review before expansion.

## Preserve the reasoning chain

Trace meaningful claims as:

**Customer evidence -> buyer insight -> alternative and decision criterion ->
capability -> differentiated value -> position -> message -> sales action.**

Use Sourced, Assumed or Gap for every link. A capability is not differentiation
until a buyer cares about the difference and an alternative or status quo is
named. A company asset or relationship can be a right-to-win advantage when the
account access and buying relevance are evidenced. Do not imply exclusivity.

## Competitive win conditions

Before selecting a broad position, compare two or three credible territories.
Use customer relevance, alternatives, meaningful difference, product readiness,
proof, commercial implications and strategic trade-offs. Select one and state
what evidence would change the choice. The final statement remains a concise
label; retain the complete customer/problem/alternative/value/proof architecture
under it so brevity does not erase the reason to choose.

For broad work, carry a compact table with: alternative or status quo, why it is
attractive, buyer criterion, proposed advantage, proof required, and win, loss or
disqualify condition. Keep unknown proof as a Gap. This turns competitive facts
into a commercial decision without inventing win rates.

## Handoff rules

Positioning owns company and solution positioning within the market and
beachhead scope inherited from their owners. Messaging owns expression and
must not add a differentiator that positioning did not approve or propose.
Messaging should carry the relevant persona, alternative, proof and sales action
into each pillar. Narrow requests may use only the named product, use case and
scope; do not reopen discovery when the user has already fixed them.
